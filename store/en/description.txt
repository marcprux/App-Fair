App Fair is an open, privacy-respecting app store. Browse free and open-source apps, read their details, and install or update them straight from the repositories you trust — no account, no tracking, no ads.

FEATURES

• Browse F-Droid-compatible catalogs, with search, categories and sorting
• Read what an app actually asks for — permissions, anti-features and its signing key — before installing
• Install and update through Android's own installer, after the download is verified against the checksum the catalog published
• Add, enable, disable and remove catalogs: the sources are yours to choose
• Filter out anti-features you do not want to see

PRIVACY

App Fair has no account and collects nothing about you. It talks to the catalogs you have enabled and to nothing else.

HOW INSTALLS WORK

App Fair does not install anything silently. It downloads the package, checks it against the catalog's published checksum, and hands it to the system installer — the same confirmation you would see installing any app from any source.

Open source: github.com/marcprux/App-Fair
